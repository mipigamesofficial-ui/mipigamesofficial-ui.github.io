Juicy Slide

Slide the fruit. Pop the pairs.

  • 300 levels in 15 themed worlds, boards of many shapes
  • Endless mode where the board grows from 3x3 to 6x6
  • 20 original fruits to discover; original art and music
  • Google AdMob integrated (banner, interstitial, rewarded) - currently with test IDs
  • Android, targetSdk 36; plays offline

Status: finished, not yet published on Google Play. The complete project (source code) is offered for sale.
