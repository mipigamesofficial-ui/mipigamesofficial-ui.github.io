Juicy Slide — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Developer: MiPe Games, Serbia

Contact: mipigamesofficial@gmail.com

1. Summary

Juicy Slide is a fruit-merging puzzle game that works offline. **We (the developer) do not collect, store on

servers, sell or receive any personal data.** Your game progress stays on your device.

The game shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. Google's advertising software inside the app

collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described in section 3.

2. Data stored only on your device

The game saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • settings (music, sound effects, vibration, control style)
  • game progress (the saved Endless game, best scores, level progress and stars, hearts, bombs, daily basket streak)
  • statistics (games played, merges, walls destroyed, total play time, fruits discovered)
  • awards (achievements) and tutorial status
  • advertising frequency counters (how many levels or games since the last full-screen ad, and when it was shown)

You can delete all of this at any time with Settings → Reset progress (settings are kept), or by

clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), operated by

Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. According to Google's published disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK,

the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

DataExamplePurpose (per Google)
IP addressused to estimate approximate (general) locationadvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
User product interactionsapp launches, taps, ad/video viewsadvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostic informationapp/SDK performance such as launch time and hang rateanalytics, fraud prevention
Device and account identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set IDadvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

Google states this data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google's use of this data is governed by Google's

policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ad formats used: a banner on the level map screen (never over the game board), full-screen ads shown

occasionally between levels or games (never during play), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch

for a bonus (extra moves, a heart or a bomb).

Your choices:

  • In regions where consent is required: when you open the app, Google's consent form may ask whether you

    • agree to personalised advertising and data use. You can change your choice later in

    Settings → Privacy choices (ads) (shown when it applies to you).

  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings

    • (Settings → Privacy → Ads, wording varies by device).

  • The game is fully playable without ever watching an optional rewarded ad. If ads cannot load (for example

    • offline), the game works normally.

Ad content setting: ads are requested with the maximum ad content rating set to "General audiences".

4. Children

Juicy Slide is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

The app does not ask for any age or personal details.

5. Permissions

The app requests no runtime ("dangerous") permissions. It uses: internet and network-state access (to

load ads), vibration (haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings), and permissions added by Google's ads

SDK for the advertising ID and Android's ad-services APIs.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data and do not share any data ourselves. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK

is processed by Google as described above.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data on servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. On-device data is deleted as described in

section 2. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy and your Google account / device ad

settings.

8. Analytics

This version contains no analytics SDK (no Firebase, no third-party tracking). If analytics are added in

a later version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

9. Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. The "Last updated" date shows the latest

version.
